Stream Golden Clips Uploader

Stream Golden Clips Uploader is a private tool used by the owner of the Stream Golden Clips channels (YouTube @streamgoldenclips, Instagram @streamgolden.clips). It turns approved footage from creator clipping campaigns into short vertical clips and, after the owner has watched and approved each clip, uploads it to the owner's own channels.

What it does

  • Cuts short clips from videos that creators and brands provide for clipping campaigns.
  • Shows every clip to the owner for review. Nothing is uploaded without the owner's approval.
  • Uploads approved clips to the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API (YouTube API Services) and to the owner's own Instagram account through the Instagram API.
  • Reads basic channel information (channel name and statistics) to show which account is connected.

The tool is not offered to the public and is only used with the owner's own accounts.
