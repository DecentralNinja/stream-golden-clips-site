Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 November 2026

This policy explains what data Stream Golden Clips Uploader ("the app") accesses, how it is used, and how access can be removed. The app is a private tool used only by the owner of the Stream Golden Clips channels to manage their own accounts.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Data the app accesses

  • YouTube (with your permission through Google sign-in):
    • View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly): the channel name, handle, ID and public statistics (subscriber, video and view counts), used to show which channel is connected and how uploaded clips perform.
    • Manage your YouTube videos (youtube.upload): used only to upload short videos that the channel owner has reviewed and approved in the app.
  • Instagram (through Meta's Instagram API): the username and the permission to publish Reels, used only to publish clips the owner has approved.

The app does not access any other Google, YouTube or Instagram data, such as private messages, contacts or other people's accounts.

How data is stored

  • Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are not sent to any server run by the app, and the app has no server or database of its own.
  • Channel information is requested when needed and not kept for longer than 30 days.
  • The app does not use cookies, advertising, analytics or tracking, and does not sell, rent or share any data with anyone.

How to remove access and delete data

  • Use the Disconnect button in the app. It revokes the app's access at Google and deletes the saved sign-in token.
  • Or remove the app at any time in your Google account permissions (security settings: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions).
  • Instagram access can be removed in Instagram's Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
